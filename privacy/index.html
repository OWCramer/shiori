Shiori

Privacy Policy

Effective 16 October 2026

Shiori is a player for the visual novels you own. It’s made by Owen Cramer, an independent developer (“I” below).

The short version: I don’t collect anything. Shiori has no account of its own, no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. What you sign in with and what you play stays on your devices, in your iCloud, and with the services you choose to use.

What Shiori stores, and where

On your device

  • The games you download, their patches and their saves, in Shiori’s folder (visible in the Files app).
  • A cache of your library, artwork, the compatibility and patch lists, and series information, so the app works offline.
  • Your settings, and a short connection log for troubleshooting. The log never contains passwords or tokens and never leaves your device.

In your iCloud

Apple’s iCloud, under your Apple Account. I can’t access it.

  • iCloud Keychain: your Steam sign-in token, your SteamGridDB API key if you add one, and your library choices (games added or hidden, collections, artwork picks, each game’s version choice, per-game options and the last-played record). This is what lets you sign in once and see the same library on your iPhone, iPad and Apple TV. Signing out removes the token from every device.
  • iCloud Drive: each game’s saves, so they follow you between devices.

Turning off iCloud Keychain or iCloud Drive for Shiori keeps everything on the one device.

Services Shiori talks to

Shiori connects directly from your device to these services. Each receives your IP address and the request itself, as with any internet connection, and handles it under its own privacy policy. Shiori sends them nothing else about you.

What Shiori doesn’t do

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind, and no third-party SDKs that do them.
  • No Shiori account, and no server of mine that your data passes through.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera. Bluetooth is used only to connect game controllers.

Crash reports

If you’ve chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device’s settings, Apple may send me anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. That’s Apple’s system and your choice; Shiori adds nothing to it.

Adult content

Adult patches, adult downloadable content and adult artwork appear in Shiori only when your Steam account’s own store preferences allow adult content. Shiori reads that preference from Steam and never offers a switch of its own.

Children

Shiori is rated 18+ and isn’t directed at children. I don’t knowingly collect information from anyone, children included.

Deleting your data

  • Games and saves on a device: delete a game from its page, or delete Shiori.
  • Synced settings and the Steam sign-in: sign out in Settings (this signs out every device), or remove Shiori’s items from iCloud Keychain.
  • iCloud saves: Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage › Shiori.
  • Steam: remove the Shiori device from your account’s authorized devices on Steam; Steam Cloud saves are managed by Steam.
  • Denpasoft: Settings › Denpasoft › Sign Out.

There’s nothing to delete on my side, because I hold nothing.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at owcramer.github.io/shiori/privacy with a new effective date.
